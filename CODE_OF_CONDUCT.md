# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**Be respectful.** Welcome people of every background and experience level. Critique ideas, not people.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults or personal attacks, discriminatory language or imagery, sharing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. Report problems privately to the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/AbdulWaheedorg) or, for anything sensitive, through [private reporting](https://github.com/AbdulWaheedorg/Zero-Cost-Gemini/security/advisories/new).
